This paper examines air temperature, as the weather variable with patterns that are easy to observe and explain. Over a year the temperature rises to a peak in the summer and drops to the lowest point in the winter, and over a single day it warms up after sunrise and cools back down overnight. To look at these seasonal and daily cycles, hourly temperatures for Akron, Ohio, were collected from July 2004 to November 2026.

For every day in the data, the mean temperature and the standard deviation of that day's hourly readings were calculated. The daily mean follows the pattern of the seasonal cycle, while the daily standard deviation shows how much the temperature shifts during the day.
